About the scores and data

Dice measures overlap between the predicted and reference regions on a scale from 0 to 1. The whole tumour includes all three labels. The core combines enhancing and non-enhancing tumour.

Boundary recovery is a frozen experimental add-on. Mint highlights additions, not confirmed oedema. Use Differences to compare the selected prediction with the annotation. These are the 51 held-out content-group representatives from the retrained experiment.

The viewer uses the saved masks on the same voxel grid. MRI brightness is scaled for display. The 3D views show exposed voxel faces. All scores use the original label arrays.

Data: Medical Segmentation Decathlon and contributors, Task01 BrainTumour. Derived visualizations are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0; arrays are reoriented, brightness scaled, and predictions/annotations coloured. Research and teaching viewer, developed with AI assistance.
